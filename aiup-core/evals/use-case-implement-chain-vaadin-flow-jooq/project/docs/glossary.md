# Glossary

| Term               | Definition                                                                      | Avoid                |
|--------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------|----------------------|
| Plot               | A numbered piece of land in the garden that one gardener works for a season.    | Allotment, Parcel    |
| Gardener           | A member of the garden who works one or more plots.                             | Tenant, Member       |
| Garden Coordinator | The volunteer in the garden office who allocates the plots.                     | Admin                |
| Plot Assignment    | The allocation of one plot to one gardener for one season.                      | Lease, Rental        |
| Season             | A calendar year of gardening; the current season is the year of today's date.   | Year                 |
| Closed Plot        | A plot that is not offered for assignment, for example while its soil recovers. | Blocked plot         |
