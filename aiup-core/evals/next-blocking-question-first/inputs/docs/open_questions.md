## OQ-001: Does the reminder go out on Sundays?

**Element:** UC-004 BR-001
**Raised by:** /use-case-implement, 2026-10-12
**Blocking:** Yes
**Status:** Open

The library is closed on Sundays. BR-001 counts calendar days; a reminder due on a Sunday could go out on the Saturday
before or the Monday after.
