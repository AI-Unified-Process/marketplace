# Glossary

| Term      | Definition                                                                 | Avoid            |
|-----------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------|------------------|
| Member    | A registered person who may borrow copies of books.                        | Customer, Client |
| Librarian | An employee who registers members and records loans and returns.           |                  |
| Copy      | One physical exemplar of a book title that can be lent.                    | Item             |
| Loan      | The record that a member has borrowed a copy, with its loan and due date.  | Rental           |
