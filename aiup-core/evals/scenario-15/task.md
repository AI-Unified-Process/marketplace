# Book Loan Business Process with Return

## Problem/Feature Description

A city library's lending system follows the AI Unified Process. The requirements catalog is in `docs/requirements.md`,
the use case diagram in `docs/use_cases.puml`, and the specified use cases in `docs/use_cases/`. There are no process
models in the project yet.

The business analysts want the complete book loan business process as a BPMN 2.0 model: a member reserves a title,
a librarian lends an available copy or the member joins the waiting list, and a lent book is eventually returned at
the counter. The QA team will derive end-to-end test cases from the model.

Model the "Book Loan" business process, including the return of the book.

## Output Specification

Either produce the BPMN 2.0 process model under `docs/processes/`, or, if the specification is not ready for it,
write a short report to `docs/process-model-report.md` that explains what is missing and what has to happen first.
