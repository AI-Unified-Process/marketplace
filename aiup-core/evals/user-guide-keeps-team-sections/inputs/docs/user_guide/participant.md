# Participant

This chapter is for participants, who register for workshops and attend them.

## Register for a workshop
<!-- UC-002 -->

Reserve a place in an upcoming workshop so that you can attend it.

**Before you start:** At least one workshop that starts in the future exists.

1. Open the list of upcoming workshops. You see each workshop with its title, start time, and free places.
2. Select a workshop and enter your name and email.
3. Confirm the registration. You see the message "You are registered for <workshop title>."

**If the workshop is fully booked:** You see the message "This workshop is fully booked." Choose another workshop.

**If the workshop starts in less than 24 hours:** You see the message "Registration for this workshop has closed."

**If you are already registered with this email:** You see the message "You are already registered for this
workshop."

**Good to know:** A workshop takes registrations only while it has free places. Registration closes 24 hours before
the workshop starts. You can register with the same email only once per workshop.

**Result:** Your registration is stored with your name, your email, and the time you registered, and the workshop has
one free place less.

## Signing in with your company account

Employees of Example Corp do not need a separate password. On the start page, click **Sign in with Microsoft** and
use your company email. External participants keep using the email link they received with their invitation.

## Cancel your registration
<!-- UC-004 -->

Give back your place in a workshop when you can no longer attend, so that another participant can take it.

**Before you start:** You have a registration for a workshop that starts in the future.

1. Open the list of your registrations. You see each registration with the workshop title and start time.
2. Select a registration and choose to cancel it.
3. Confirm the cancellation. You see the message "Your registration for <workshop title> is cancelled."

**If the workshop starts in less than 24 hours:** The cancellation is rejected and your registration stays.

**If you do not confirm:** Your registration stays and you see the list of your registrations again.

**Good to know:** You can cancel a registration until 24 hours before the workshop starts.

**Result:** Your registration no longer exists, and the workshop has one free place more.
