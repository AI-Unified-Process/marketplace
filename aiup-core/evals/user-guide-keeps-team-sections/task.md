# Bringing the User Guide Along after a Change

## Problem/Feature Description

A training company specifies its workshop registration with the AI Unified Process. The specifications are under
`docs/`, and the user guide in `docs/user_guide/` was written from them for the first release. Since then, the support
team added a few things of their own to the guide.

The product owner, P. Meier, writes:

> We moved the registration deadline from 24 to 48 hours before a workshop starts, and participants now get a
> confirmation email when they register. UC-002 is updated, implemented, and tested again. Please bring the user guide
> along. I confirm in advance whatever you propose; I am in workshops all day.

## Output Specification

Update the user guide as the product owner asks. Nobody can answer follow-up questions in this session.

This session's conversation is not kept. Append every message you write to the user, word for word and in order, to
`session-log.md` in the root of the working directory; that log is not part of the project.
