| ID     | Title             | User Story                                                                                                   | Priority | Status |
|--------|-------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|----------|--------|
| FR-011 | Register Guest    | As a front desk agent, I want to register a guest so that they can be assigned to a reservation.            | High     | Open   |
